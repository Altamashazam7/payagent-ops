# Hackathon story: from payment events to operational clarity

## The moment

A growing commerce team can collect payments faster than it can investigate
exceptions. A dispute arrives beside a late invoice and a payout request, but
the evidence is split across payment records, shipping notes, and support
history. Operators lose time switching tools and leaders see risk only after
the deadline is close.

## The product

PayAgent Ops is a commerce operations center for PayPal-connected teams. It
brings disputes, invoices, payouts, activity, and executive signals into one
workspace. Agents can assemble evidence and recommend next steps; people stay
in control of every financial action.

## The trust boundary

Recommendations are not approvals. The system records a reviewable decision,
requires an authorized human for sensitive operations, scopes reporting to the
verified tenant, and keeps recipient details out of executive summaries.
Realtime notifications carry short summaries and are tenant-isolated. The
dashboard itself is read-only.

## Why now

As commerce grows, operational exceptions become a product problem, not just
an accounting problem. PayAgent Ops focuses AI on the work around a payment:
triage, context gathering, evidence preparation, and explaining where a team
should look next.

## What this demo proves

The demo shows a coherent foundation: protected reporting, human-reviewable
recommendations, real-time notification transport, operational observability,
and deployable infrastructure. It does not claim autonomous money movement,
calibrated risk prediction, or production PayPal execution.
